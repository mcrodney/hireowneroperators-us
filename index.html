Why CW

Put your truck to work with a carrier that has been here since 1990

CW Leasing & Transport Inc is a family-owned carrier out of South Bend, Indiana. For more than three decades we have moved freight that most carriers never see: concert and tour equipment, stadium merchandise trailers, and event units, along with power only and towaway moves.

We are looking for owner operators who take pride in their truck and want to haul steady entertainment and power only freight across 49 states and Canada.
